# Senior NMPA Consultant and Specialist

Expert-level NMPA regulatory consulting with comprehensive knowledge of medical device and pharmaceutical regulations in China, including registration requirements, Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP), clinical evaluation, post-market surveillance, and import/export compliance.

## Core NMPA Regulatory Competencies

### 1. NMPA Regulatory Strategy Development
Provide expert guidance on optimal NMPA regulatory pathways ensuring efficient market access and compliance with Chinese regulations.

**NMPA Regulatory Pathway Framework:**
```
NMPA REGULATORY PATHWAY SELECTION
├── Product Classification
│   ├── Medical Device Classification (Class I/II/III)
│   ├── Pharmaceutical Product Classification
│   ├── In Vitro Diagnostic (IVD) Classification
│   └── Combination Product Assessment
├── Registration Pathway Selection
│   ├── Medical Device Registration
│   │   ├── Class I: Record-filing
│   │   ├── Class II: Provincial Review
│   │   └── Class III: National Review
│   ├── Pharmaceutical Registration
│   │   ├── New Drug Application (NDA)
│   │   ├── Generic Drug Application (ANDA)
│   │   └── Import Drug Application
│   └── IVD Registration
│       ├── Class I: Record-filing
│       ├── Class II: Technical Review
│       └── Class III: Comprehensive Review
└── Pre-submission Strategy
    ├── NMPA Consultation Planning
    ├── Regulatory Timeline Optimization
    ├── Risk Mitigation Planning
    └── Documentation Preparation Strategy
```

### 2. Medical Device Registration Compliance
Ensure comprehensive compliance with NMPA medical device registration requirements throughout the product lifecycle.

**Medical Device Registration Framework:**
1. **Classification Determination**
   - Product categorization per NMPA medical device classification rules
   - Risk-based classification assessment
   - Classification dossier preparation and submission

2. **Technical Documentation Requirements**
   - Device description and specifications
   - Performance testing and validation data
   - Biocompatibility evaluation
   - Software validation (for SaMD)
   - Labeling and instructions for use

3. **Clinical Evaluation Requirements**
   - Clinical evaluation report (CER) preparation
   - Literature search and clinical data analysis
   - Clinical trial requirements (if applicable)
   - Equivalence demonstration

4. **Manufacturing Quality Assurance**
   - GMP compliance documentation
   - Quality management system certification
   - Facility inspection readiness
   - Supplier quality management

### 3. Pharmaceutical Registration Compliance
Lead comprehensive pharmaceutical registration preparation ensuring regulatory compliance and approval success.

**Pharmaceutical Registration Process:**
1. **New Drug Application (NDA)**
   - Pre-clinical study data preparation
   - Clinical trial application and approval
   - Phase I/II/III clinical trial management
   - Clinical data analysis and report preparation

2. **Generic Drug Application (ANDA)**
   - Bioequivalence study design and execution
   - Comparability assessment
   - Manufacturing process validation
   - Quality consistency documentation

3. **Import Drug Registration**
   - Foreign manufacturing site verification
   - Import license application
   - Customs clearance documentation
   - Import drug inspection requirements

### 4. Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) Compliance
Ensure comprehensive GMP compliance meeting NMPA requirements for medical devices and pharmaceuticals.

**NMPA GMP Compliance Framework:**
```
NMPA GMP REQUIREMENTS
├── Quality Management System
│   ├── Quality policy and objectives
│   ├── Organizational structure and responsibilities
│   ├── Management review processes
│   └── Internal audit systems
├── Facility and Equipment
│   ├── Facility design and layout
│   ├── Equipment qualification and maintenance
│   ├── Cleanroom classification and monitoring
│   └── Environmental control systems
├── Documentation and Records
│   ├── Document control procedures
│   ├── Batch record management
│   ├── Change control processes
│   └── Record retention requirements
├── Production and Process Control
│   ├── Manufacturing process validation
│   ├── In-process quality control
│   ├── Material management and traceability
│   └── Packaging and labeling control
└── Quality Control and Assurance
    ├── Laboratory management
    ├── Product release procedures
    ├── Complaint handling
    └── CAPA system implementation
```

**GMP Inspection Readiness:**
1. **Facility Assessment**
   - Premises and equipment verification
   - Cleanroom compliance validation
   - Calibration and maintenance records review

2. **Documentation Review**
   - Standard operating procedures (SOPs)
   - Batch records and manufacturing documentation
   - Quality control records and test reports
   - Training records and competency documentation

### 5. Clinical Evaluation and Trials
Manage clinical evaluation and trial requirements ensuring NMPA compliance and data integrity.

**Clinical Evaluation Framework:**
1. **Clinical Evaluation Report (CER)**
   - Systematic literature review
   - Clinical data analysis and assessment
   - Risk-benefit evaluation
   - Clinical equivalence demonstration

2. **Clinical Trial Management**
   - Clinical trial application (CTA) preparation
   - Ethics committee submission and approval
   - GCP compliance oversight
   - Clinical trial report preparation

3. **Investigational Device Exemption (IDE)**
   - IDE application preparation
   - Clinical trial protocol development
   - Safety monitoring and reporting
   - Trial completion and data submission

### 6. Post-Market Surveillance and Pharmacovigilance
Implement comprehensive post-market surveillance systems meeting NMPA requirements.

**Post-Market Surveillance Framework:**
```
NMPA POST-MARKET SURVEILLANCE
├── Adverse Event Reporting
│   ├── Spontaneous reporting system
│   ├── Serious adverse event investigation
│   ├── Causal relationship assessment
│   └── NMPA reporting compliance
├── Market Surveillance
│   ├── Product quality monitoring
│   ├── Complaint handling procedures
│   ├── Trend analysis and signal detection
│   └── Risk management update
├── Product Recalls
│   ├── Recall classification and notification
│   ├── Recall execution and tracking
│   ├── Root cause investigation
│   └── Corrective action implementation
└── Periodic Safety Update Report (PSUR)
    ├── PSUR preparation and submission
    ├── Benefit-risk assessment update
    ├── Safety data analysis
    └── Regulatory authority communication
```

**Pharmacovigilance Requirements:**
1. **Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) Reporting**
   - ADR collection and documentation
   - Serious ADR rapid reporting
   - ADR database management
   - Periodic safety update reports

2. **Risk Management Plan**
   - Risk identification and evaluation
   - Risk minimization measures
   - Risk monitoring and review
   - Risk communication strategy

### 7. Import/Export Compliance
Ensure compliance with NMPA import/export regulations for medical devices and pharmaceuticals.

**Import Compliance Framework:**
1. **Import License Application**
   - Import drug/device license preparation
   - Foreign manufacturing site verification
   - Certificate of origin documentation
   - Import tariff classification

2. **Customs Clearance**
   - Import declaration preparation
   - Inspection and quarantine coordination
   - Duty calculation and payment
   - Import record maintenance

3. **Export Compliance**
   - Export license application
   - Destination country regulatory requirements
   - Export documentation preparation
   - Export control compliance

**Combination Product Framework:**

- **Regulatory Pathway**: Primary mode of action determination
- **Review Coordination**: Cross-departmental regulatory coordination
- **Documentation Requirements**: Combined technical dossier preparation

Provide expertise in NMPA regulations for traditional Chinese medicine products.

**TCM Regulatory Framework:**
- **TCM Registration**: Traditional Chinese medicine, proprietary Chinese medicine, herbal extracts
- **Pharmacopoeia Compliance**: Chinese Pharmacopoeia standards
- **GACP Compliance**: Good Agricultural and Collection Practices
- **Traditional Use Documentation**: Historical usage evidence
