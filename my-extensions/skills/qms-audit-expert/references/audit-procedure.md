# Internal Audit Procedure

**Document Number:** SOP-8.2.4  
**Revision:** 01  
**Effective Date:** YYYY-MM-DD  

## 1. PURPOSE

To establish a systematic process for planning, conducting, and documenting internal audits of the Quality Management System (QMS) to ensure compliance with ISO 13485:2016 and applicable regulatory requirements.

## 2. SCOPE

This procedure applies to all internal audits conducted within [Company Name], including:
- QMS internal audits
- Process audits
- Product audits
- Supplier audits

## 3. REFERENCES

- ISO 13485:2016 Clause 8.2.4
- SOP-4.2.4 Control of Documents
- SOP-4.2.5 Control of Records
- SOP-8.5.2 Corrective Action

## 4. DEFINITIONS

- **Internal Audit:** A systematic, independent examination of the QMS to determine whether quality activities and related results comply with planned arrangements.
- **Auditor:** A person trained and qualified to conduct audits.
- **Auditee:** The organization or department being audited.
- **Nonconformity:** Non-fulfillment of a requirement.

## 5. RESPONSIBILITIES

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Quality Manager | Overall responsibility for internal audit program |
| Audit Program Manager | Plan and coordinate audits |
| Lead Auditor | Lead audit team, prepare report |
| Auditors | Conduct audits, document findings |
| Auditee | Provide access, respond to findings |

## 6. PROCEDURE

### 6.1 Audit Program Planning

1. **Annual Audit Schedule:** The Audit Program Manager develops an annual audit schedule based on:
   - Previous audit results
   - Risk assessment
   - Process importance
   - Changes to QMS or regulations

2. **Audit Plan:** For each audit, the Lead Auditor prepares an audit plan including:
   - Audit objectives
   - Scope
   - Criteria (ISO 13485, procedures, regulations)
   - Audit team
   - Schedule
   - Resources needed

3. **Approval:** Audit plan is approved by Quality Manager.

### 6.2 Audit Preparation

1. **Auditor Selection:** Select competent auditors independent of the area being audited.

2. **Audit Checklist:** Develop audit checklist based on audit criteria.

3. **Notification:** Notify auditee of audit at least [X] working days in advance.

### 6.3 Audit Execution

1. **Opening Meeting:** Conduct opening meeting with auditee to:
   - Review audit plan
   - Confirm scope and objectives
   - Introduce audit team

2. **On-Site Activities:**
   - Review documents and records
   - Conduct interviews
   - Observe processes
   - Collect objective evidence

3. **Findings Documentation:** Document findings (conformities and nonconformities).

4. **Closing Meeting:** Conduct closing meeting to:
   - Present findings
   - Discuss nonconformities
   - Obtain auditee feedback

### 6.4 Nonconformity Classification

| Severity | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Major** | Noncompliance that could affect product safety or regulatory compliance |
| **Minor** | Noncompliance that does not affect product safety |
| **Observation** | Opportunity for improvement, not a nonconformity |

### 6.5 Audit Report

1. **Report Preparation:** Lead Auditor prepares audit report within [X] working days.

2. **Report Content:**
   - Audit objectives and scope
   - Audit team and dates
   - Summary of findings
   - Detailed nonconformity reports
   - Conclusions and recommendations

3. **Report Distribution:** Distribute report to relevant management.

### 6.6 Corrective Action Follow-up

1. **CAPA Request:** Nonconformities trigger CAPA per SOP-8.5.2.

2. **Follow-up Audit:** Conduct follow-up to verify corrective actions.

3. **Closure:** Close audit when all corrective actions are verified.

### 6.7 Records Retention

Audit records are retained per SOP-4.2.5 for [X] years.
