## PMS Overview

### Definition
Post-market surveillance is a systematic process to proactively collect and review experience gained from devices placed on the market.

### Purpose
- Confirm safety and performance in normal use
- Identify risks and benefits
- Identify areas for improvement
- Detect trends and signals

### Legal Basis
- Article 83: General obligations
- Article 84: PMS plan
- Article 85: PMS reports
- Article 86: Periodic safety update reports
- Article 87-92: Vigilance requirements

## PMS Plan Requirements

#### 1. Proactive Surveillance
- Systematic process for collecting data

#### 2. Reactive Surveillance

### PMS Metrics

#### Key Performance Indicators
- Complaint rate

- Trend data

## Vigilance Requirements

### Article 87: Serious Incidents

#### Definition of Serious Incident
- Death of patient/user
- Unanticipated serious deterioration in health
- Risk of death/serious deterioration

#### Reporting Requirements
- Report to competent authority without undue delay
- Within 15 days for serious incidents
- Within 2 days for public health emergencies
- Follow-up report within reasonable time

#### Report Content
- Device identification
- Incident description
- Root cause analysis
- Corrective actions
- Benefit-risk determination

### Article 88: Trend Reporting

#### When Required
- Significant increase in incidents
- Unexpected incidents
- Signals of emerging risks

#### Assessment Requirements
- Statistical analysis
- Comparison with expected rates
- Root cause investigation

### Article 89: Field Safety Corrective Actions (FSCAs)

#### Definition
Corrective action taken to reduce risk of serious incident

#### Examples
- Device recall
- Modification
- Software update
- IFU revision

#### Notification Requirements
- Notify users via Field Safety Notice (FSN)
- Report to competent authority
- Document in EUDAMED

### Article 90: Grave Risks

#### Immediate Notification
- Devices posing grave risk
- Corrective action immediately required
- Prohibition on use/marketing

## PSUR Requirements

### Article 86: Periodic Safety Update Report

#### Devices Requiring PSUR
- Class III and IIb: Every 2 years
- Class IIa: Every 5 years (if Notified Body involved)
- Class I: Summary of PMS data

#### PSUR Content

##### Section 1: Device Summary
- Device identification
- Intended purpose
- Classification
- Manufacturing information

##### Section 2: PMS Data Summary
- Sales data
- Production data
- Complaints received
- Incidents reported
- FSCAs conducted

##### Section 3: Incident Analysis
- Serious incidents
- Non-serious incidents
- Trend analysis
- Root causes
- CAPA taken

##### Section 4: Benefit-Risk Conclusion
- Benefit assessment
- Risk assessment
- Benefit-risk ratio
- Residual risk acceptability

##### Section 5: PMCF Conclusions
- PMCF activities completed
- Results summary
- Impact on CER
- Recommendations

##### Section 6: Overall Conclusions
- Safety conclusion
- Risk acceptability
- Effectiveness of measures
- Recommendations

### PSUR Distribution
- Submit to Notified Body
- Make available to competent authority
- Class I devices: Summary only required

## PMCF Requirements

### Article 74 and Annex XIV Part B

### PMCF Purpose
- Confirm safety and performance
- Identify previously unknown risks
- Update benefit-risk assessment
- Identify systematic misuse

### PMCF Plan

#### Plan Content
- Objectives of PMCF
- Methods to be used
- Data sources
- Milestones
- Evaluation criteria
- Integration with PMS

#### PMCF Methods
- PMCF studies
- Registry participation
- Literature review
- Post-market investigations
- Real-world evidence

### PMCF Evaluation Report

#### Report Content
- Summary of activities
- Results and analysis
- Conclusions
- Impact on CER
- Recommendations

#### Timeline
- Updated as activities complete
- Part of PSUR
- Submitted to NB

### PMCF vs. PMS Distinction

| Aspect | PMS | PMCF |
|--------|-----|------|
| Focus | General surveillance | Clinical data collection |
| Methods | Various | Clinical focus |
| Output | Overall safety picture | Clinical evidence update |
| Requirement | All devices | Devices requiring ongoing clinical evidence |

## Common PMS Deficiencies

### Top Issues Identified

1. **Inadequate PMS Plans**
   - Not comprehensive
   - No proactive data collection
   - Responsibilities unclear

2. **Vigilance Failures**
   - Delayed reporting
   - Incomplete reports
   - Trend analysis lacking

3. **PSUR Gaps**
   - Data incomplete
   - Benefit-risk not updated
   - PMCF integration lacking

4. **PMCF Issues**
   - Activities not defined
   - No evaluation criteria
   - Results not integrated

### Best Practices

1. **Integrate PMS into Quality System**
   - Clear responsibilities
   - Documented procedures
   - Regular review

2. **Use Risk-Based Approach**
   - Higher risk = more intensive PMS
   - Adjust based on experience

3. **Maintain Continuous Vigilance**
   - Monitor all sources
   - Analyze trends
   - Take timely action

4. **Keep Records Current**
   - Update PSUR regularly
   - Integrate new data into CER
   - Document all decisions
