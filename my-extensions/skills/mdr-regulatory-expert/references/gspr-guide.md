## GSPR Overview

### Purpose
The GSPR establish the essential requirements that devices must meet to obtain CE marking under MDR.

### Structure
- **Chapter 1**: General requirements (Articles 1-9)
- **Chapter 2**: Design and manufacturing requirements (Articles 10-22)
- **Chapter 3**: Performance and safety requirements (Articles 23-29)
- **Chapter 4**: Requirements for devices with software (Article 17)
- **Chapter 5**: Labeling requirements (Article 23)

## Chapter 1: General Requirements

### Article 1: Risk Management
- Devices shall not compromise clinical condition or safety
- Devices shall not expose patients to unacceptable risks
- Benefits must outweigh residual risks
- Risk management integrated throughout lifecycle

### Article 2: Intended Purpose
- Devices shall perform as intended
- Labeling shall not mislead
- Devices shall achieve intended purpose

### Article 3: Device Lifecycle
- Devices shall be designed for entire lifecycle
- Safety shall not be compromised by aging
- Lifetime considerations documented

### Article 4: Reuse
- Reusable devices shall be designed for safe reuse
- Reprocessing instructions provided
- Validation of reprocessing

### Article 5: Safety Principles
- State of the art considerations
- Acceptable risk profile
- Safety built into design

### Article 6: Demonstrating Conformity
- Solutions adopted must be documented
- Justification for risk acceptance
- State of the art alignment

### Article 7: Performance Characteristics
- Performance defined and validated
- Suitable analytical/clinical methods
- Performance suitable for intended use

### Article 8: Knowledge Basis
- Decisions based on knowledge
- Relevant data considered
- Knowledge documented

### Article 9: Appropriate Lifetime
- Lifetime documented
- Aging effects considered
- Degradation acceptable
