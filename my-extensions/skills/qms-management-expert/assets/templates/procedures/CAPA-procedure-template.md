## 1. PURPOSE

To establish a systematic process for identifying, documenting, investigating, implementing, and verifying corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) to ensure nonconformities are resolved and potential issues are prevented.

## 2. SCOPE

This procedure applies to all nonconformities identified within [Company Name], including but not limited to:
- Product nonconformities
- Process nonconformities
- Customer complaints
- Internal audit findings
- External audit findings
- Regulatory observations
- Product recalls
- Adverse events

## 3. REFERENCES

- ISO 13485:2016 Clause 8.5.2 and 8.5.3
- SOP-4.2.4 Control of Documents
- SOP-4.2.5 Control of Records
- SOP-8.2.2 Complaint Handling
- SOP-8.2.4 Internal Audit

## 4. DEFINITIONS

- **Corrective Action:** Action taken to eliminate the cause of a detected nonconformity or other undesirable situation.
- **Preventive Action:** Action taken to eliminate the cause of a potential nonconformity or other undesirable situation.
- **Nonconformity:** Non-fulfillment of a requirement.
- **Root Cause:** The fundamental reason for the occurrence of a nonconformity.

## 5. RESPONSIBILITIES

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Quality Manager | Overall responsibility for CAPA system |
| Originator | Identify and report nonconformity |
| Investigator | Conduct root cause analysis |
| CAPA Coordinator | Track and manage CAPA process |
| Department Head | Approve and implement corrective/preventive actions |
| Management Representative | Review CAPA effectiveness |

## 6. PROCEDURE

### 6.1 Identification of Nonconformities

Nonconformities may be identified from:
- Customer complaints
- Internal audits
- External audits (certification, regulatory)
- Product inspections and testing
- Process monitoring
- Supplier performance
- Trend analysis
- Risk management outputs
- Management review

### 6.2 CAPA Initiation

1. **Complete CAPA Form:** The originator completes the CAPA Form (CAPA-001) including:
   - Description of the nonconformity
   - Date and location of occurrence
   - Impact assessment
   - Initial containment actions

2. **Submit CAPA Form:** Submit to CAPA Coordinator within [X] working days.

3. **CAPA Log:** CAPA Coordinator logs the CAPA in the CAPA Tracking System.

### 6.3 Evaluation for Need of Action

1. **CAPA Coordinator Review:** Within [X] working days, the CAPA Coordinator evaluates:
   - Severity of the nonconformity
   - Risk to product quality and safety
   - Frequency of occurrence
   - Impact on customers and regulatory compliance

2. **Determine Action Type:**
   - **Corrective Action:** Required for actual nonconformities
   - **Preventive Action:** Required for potential nonconformities
   - **No Action:** When risk is acceptable

### 6.4 Root Cause Analysis

1. **Assign Investigator:** CAPA Coordinator assigns an investigator within [X] working days.

2. **Conduct Investigation:** Investigator uses appropriate tools:
   - 5-Why Analysis
   - Fishbone/Ishikawa Diagram
   - Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA)
   - Pareto Analysis

3. **Document Findings:** Investigator documents root cause(s) on the CAPA Form.

### 6.5 Action Planning

1. **Develop Action Plan:** Investigator develops action plan including:
   - Specific actions to address root cause
   - Responsible person(s)
   - Target completion date
   - Resources required

2. **Review and Approval:** Action plan is reviewed and approved by Department Head within [X] working days.

### 6.6 Implementation of Actions

1. **Execute Actions:** Responsible person implements actions by target date.

2. **Document Implementation:** Actions taken are documented on the CAPA Form.

3. **Notify Stakeholders:** CAPA Coordinator notifies relevant personnel of implemented changes.

### 6.7 Verification of Effectiveness

1. **Verification Plan:** CAPA Coordinator establishes verification method:
   - Inspection/testing
   - Process monitoring
   - Audit
   - Review of records

2. **Conduct Verification:** Verification is conducted within [X] working days of action completion.

3. **Document Results:** Verification results are documented on the CAPA Form.

4. **Effectiveness Determination:**
   - **Effective:** Actions successfully address root cause
   - **Not Effective:** Actions need modification; repeat process

### 6.8 CAPA Closure

1. **Review and Approval:** CAPA Coordinator reviews completed CAPA and recommends closure.

2. **Management Review:** CAPA is reviewed at next management review meeting.

3. **Close CAPA:** CAPA Coordinator closes the CAPA in the tracking system.

### 6.9 Records Retention

CAPA records are retained per SOP-4.2.5 Control of Records for [X] years or as required by regulatory requirements.
