## 1. PURPOSE

To establish and maintain control over all Quality Management System (QMS) documents to ensure that only current, approved documents are used at all points of use.

## 2. SCOPE

This procedure applies to all documents in the QMS, including:
- Policies and procedures
- Work instructions
- Forms and templates
- Quality records
- External documents (standards, regulations, customer specifications)
- Computer software documentation

## 3. REFERENCES

- ISO 13485:2016 Clause 4.2.4
- SOP-4.2.5 Control of Records
- SOP-8.5.2 Corrective Action

## 4. DEFINITIONS

- **Document:** Information and its supporting medium
- **Controlled Document:** A document that has been reviewed, approved, and distributed according to this procedure
- **Uncontrolled Copy:** A copy of a document not subject to revision control
- **Obsolete Document:** A document that is no longer current or applicable
- **External Document:** Documents from external sources (standards, regulations, customer specifications)

## 5. RESPONSIBILITIES

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Document Control Coordinator | Manage document control system |
| Document Owner | Prepare, review, and approve documents |
| Department Heads | Approve documents within their area |
| Quality Manager | Approve QMS documents |
| All Personnel | Use only approved document versions |

## 6. PROCEDURE

### 6.1 Document Identification

1. **Document Numbering System:** All QMS documents are assigned a unique identifier using the following format:
   - **Manual:** QM-XXX
   - **Procedure:** SOP-XXX
   - **Work Instruction:** WI-XXX
   - **Form:** FRM-XXX
   - **Record:** REC-XXX
   - **External Document:** EXT-XXX

2. **Document Header:** All documents must include:
   - Document title
   - Document number
   - Revision number
   - Effective date
   - Page number (Page X of Y)
   - Approval signatures

### 6.2 Document Creation

1. **Draft Preparation:** Document owner prepares draft using appropriate template.

2. **Review:** Draft is reviewed by relevant personnel for accuracy and completeness.

3. **Approval:** Document is approved by designated authority:
   - QMS procedures: Quality Manager
   - Department-specific documents: Department Head
   - External documents: Quality Manager

### 6.3 Document Distribution

1. **Distribution List:** Document Control Coordinator maintains a distribution list for each controlled document.

2. **Controlled Copies:** Controlled copies are distributed to identified locations/persons.

3. **Distribution Record:** Distribution is recorded in the Document Distribution Log (FRM-4.2.4-01).

4. **Uncontrolled Copies:** Uncontrolled copies are clearly marked "UNCONTROLLED COPY" and used for reference only.

### 6.4 Document Revision

1. **Revision Trigger:** Documents are revised when:
   - Errors are identified
   - Processes change
   - Regulatory requirements change
   - Customer requirements change
   - Audit findings require changes

2. **Revision Process:**
   - Document owner initiates revision
   - Draft is reviewed and approved
   - Revision number is incremented
   - Effective date is updated

3. **Notification:** Document Control Coordinator notifies all holders of controlled copies of the revision.

### 6.5 Document Retrieval and Replacement

1. **Old Versions:** Holders of old versions return them to Document Control Coordinator upon receiving new versions.

2. **Destruction:** Old versions are destroyed per SOP-4.2.5 Control of Records.

3. **Obsolete Documents:** Obsolete documents retained for legal or historical purposes are marked "OBSOLETE" and stored separately.

### 6.6 External Document Control

1. **Identification:** External documents are identified and tracked in the External Documents Register (FRM-4.2.4-02).

2. **Review:** External documents are reviewed for relevance and applicability.

3. **Distribution:** Relevant external documents are distributed to affected personnel.

4. **Updates:** External documents are monitored for updates and new versions are obtained as needed.

### 6.7 Document Storage

1. **Physical Documents:** Stored in secure, controlled environment with controlled access.

2. **Electronic Documents:** Stored in password-protected system with version control.

3. **Backup:** Electronic documents are backed up regularly per IT procedures.

### 6.8 Records Retention

Document control records are retained per SOP-4.2.5 Control of Records for [X] years or as required by regulatory requirements.
