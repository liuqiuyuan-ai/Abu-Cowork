# QMS Templates Directory

This directory contains templates for QMS documentation.

## Available Templates

### Quality Manual
- `quality-manual-template.md` - Complete quality manual template per ISO 13485:2016

### Procedures (Standard Operating Procedures)
- `procedures/CAPA-procedure-template.md` - Corrective and Preventive Action procedure
- `procedures/document-control-procedure-template.md` - Control of Documents procedure
- `procedures/complaint-handling-procedure-template.md` - Complaint Handling procedure
- `procedures/internal-audit-procedure-template.md` - Internal Audit procedure
- `procedures/management-review-procedure-template.md` - Management Review procedure
- `procedures/risk-management-procedure-template.md` - Risk Management procedure
- `procedures/supplier-qualification-procedure-template.md` - Supplier Qualification procedure

### Work Instructions
- `work-instructions/` - Work instruction templates for various QMS processes

### Forms and Checklists
- `forms/` - QMS forms and checklists (CAPA forms, audit checklists, etc.)

## Usage

1. Select the appropriate template for your needs
2. Replace all placeholder text (e.g., [COMPANY NAME], [DATE], [NAME])
3. Customize based on your organization's processes
4. Follow the approval workflow defined in the document control procedure
5. Ensure all required sections are completed

## Template Customization Guidelines

- Replace all bracketed placeholders with actual information
- Adjust scope and applicability to match your organization
- Add or remove sections as needed while maintaining ISO 13485 compliance
- Ensure all references to other documents are accurate
- Verify all approval signatures are obtained
- Update revision history appropriately

## Notes

- Templates are provided as starting points and should be customized
- Ensure templates align with your organization's actual processes
- Review templates periodically for updates and improvements
- Maintain version control of customized templates

For detailed guidance on creating specific documents, refer to the references/ directory.
