## Introduction to QSR

The Quality System Regulation (QSR) at 21 CFR Part 820 establishes requirements for the methods, facilities, and controls used in designing, manufacturing, packaging, labeling, storing, installing, and servicing of medical devices.

### Scope
- Applies to all finished devices intended for human use
- Applies to manufacturers, importers, and distributors
- Includes design, production, and post-market activities

## Quality System Requirements

### General Requirements (820.10)
- Establish and maintain a quality system
- Document quality policies and objectives
- Management commitment to quality

## Management Controls (820.20)

### Management Responsibility
- Quality policy established by top management
- Organizational structure defined
- Responsibilities and authorities assigned
- Management review conducted at least annually

### Quality Planning
- Establish quality objectives
- Plan quality system processes
- Document quality plan

## Design Controls (820.30)

### Design Planning
- Establish design plan
- Define design stages
- Assign responsibilities

### Design Inputs (820.30(c))
- Define device requirements
- Include user needs and intended use
- Review and approval of inputs

### Design Outputs (820.30(d))
- Document design outputs
- Ensure outputs meet input requirements
- Design outputs include device specifications

### Design Review (820.30(e))
- Conduct design reviews at appropriate stages
- Include cross-functional team
- Document review results

### Design Verification (820.30(f))
- Verify design outputs meet design inputs
- Document verification activities

### Design Validation (820.30(g))
- Validate device meets user needs
- Conduct under simulated or actual use conditions
- Document validation activities

### Design Transfer (820.30(h))
- Transfer design to production
- Ensure production can reproduce design

### Design Changes (820.30(i))
- Control design changes
- Verify and/or validate changes
- Document change process

### Design History File (DHF) (820.30(j))
- Maintain DHF for each device type
- Include all design records
- DHF is part of the Device Master Record (DMR)

## Document Controls (820.40)

### Document Approval
- Approve documents before issuance
- Review and update as necessary
- Identify revision status

### Document Distribution
- Ensure current documents available at points of use
- Remove obsolete documents
- Control external documents

## Purchasing Controls (820.50)

### Supplier Evaluation
- Establish supplier selection criteria
- Evaluate and select suppliers
- Conduct regular supplier audits

### Purchase Requirements
- Include requirements in purchase orders
- Verify purchased products meet specifications

### Receiving Inspection
- Inspect or verify purchased products
- Document inspection results

## Production Controls (820.70)

### Production and Process Controls
- Establish production processes
- Document work instructions
- Control production environment

### Device Labeling Control
- Control device labels
- Ensure accuracy of labeling
- Maintain labeling records

### Device Packaging Control
- Control device packaging
- Ensure packaging protects device
- Label packaging correctly

### Process Validation
- Validate processes affecting product quality
- Establish validation protocols
- Document validation results

### Equipment Maintenance
- Maintain production equipment
- Calibrate measuring devices
- Document maintenance activities

## Quality Assurance (820.80)

### Inspection and Testing
- Inspect incoming materials
- Test in-process products
- Final inspection before release

### Acceptance Criteria
- Establish acceptance criteria
- Document criteria in procedures
- Ensure criteria are met

### Nonconforming Product Control (820.90)
- Identify nonconforming product
- Evaluate nonconforming product
- Disposition options: rework, scrap, release with deviation

### Corrective and Preventive Actions (820.100)
- Establish CAPA system
- Investigate nonconformities
- Implement corrective actions
- Identify preventive actions

### Quality Audits (820.198)
- Conduct internal audits
- Audit quality system effectiveness
- Document audit findings

## Records Management (820.180)

### General Requirements
- Maintain required records
- Ensure records are accessible
- Protect records from damage

### Record Retention
- Retain records for device lifetime + 1 year
- Minimum retention period specified by regulation
- Electronic records must be secure and retrievable

## QMSR Transition

### What is QMSR?
- Quality Management System Regulation
- Proposed update to 21 CFR Part 820
- Harmonized with ISO 13485:2016

### Key Changes
- Consolidated DHF, DMR, DHR into Medical Device File
- Updated design control requirements
- Enhanced risk management requirements

### Transition Period
- Effective date: February 1, 2026
- Compliance date: February 1, 2027

## Audit Preparation Tips

1. **Documentation Review**: Ensure all required documents exist
2. **Record Maintenance**: Verify records are complete and accessible
3. **Training Records**: Maintain employee training documentation
4. **CAPA System**: Ensure CAPA process is effective
5. **Design Controls**: Verify design history file completeness
6. **Supplier Management**: Review supplier evaluation records

## Common FDA Inspection Findings

- Inadequate design controls
- Incomplete design history files
- Inadequate CAPA processes
- Poor document control
- Inadequate training documentation
- Incomplete complaint handling
