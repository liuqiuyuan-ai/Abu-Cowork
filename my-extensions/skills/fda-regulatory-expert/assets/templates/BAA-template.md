## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Covered Entity is a covered entity as defined in 45 CFR § 160.103;  
WHEREAS, Business Associate performs functions or activities on behalf of Covered Entity that involve the use or disclosure of Protected Health Information ("PHI");  
WHEREAS, this Agreement is required by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA") and its implementing regulations, including 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164;  

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Protected Health Information (PHI):** As defined in 45 CFR § 160.103, individually identifiable health information transmitted or maintained in any form or medium.

1.2 **Covered Entity:** As defined in 45 CFR § 160.103.

1.3 **Business Associate:** As defined in 45 CFR § 160.103.

1.4 **Breach:** As defined in 45 CFR § 164.402.

## ARTICLE 2 - OBLIGATIONS OF BUSINESS ASSOCIATE

2.1 **Safeguards:** Business Associate shall implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of PHI.

2.2 **Use and Disclosure of PHI:** Business Associate shall:
   - Use or disclose PHI only as permitted or required by this Agreement or as required by law;
   - Ensure that any subcontractors to whom it provides PHI agree to the same restrictions;
   - Not use or disclose PHI for any purpose other than the purposes specified in this Agreement.

2.3 **Breach Notification:** Business Associate shall:
   - Notify Covered Entity of any breach of unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay;
   - Assist Covered Entity in fulfilling its breach notification obligations under HIPAA.

2.4 **Records:** Business Associate shall maintain records of all disclosures of PHI as required by 45 CFR § 164.530(e).

2.5 **Access:** Business Associate shall make PHI available to Covered Entity as necessary for Covered Entity to comply with its obligations under HIPAA.

## ARTICLE 3 - OBLIGATIONS OF COVERED ENTITY

3.1 **Notice of Practices:** Covered Entity shall provide Business Associate with notice of its privacy practices as required by 45 CFR § 164.520.

3.2 **Restrictions:** Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any restrictions on the use or disclosure of PHI that Covered Entity has agreed to under 45 CFR § 164.522.

## ARTICLE 4 - TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 **Term:** This Agreement shall remain in effect until terminated as provided herein.

4.2 **Termination by Covered Entity:** Covered Entity may terminate this Agreement at any time with written notice to Business Associate.

4.3 **Termination for Breach:** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement.

4.4 **Post-Termination Obligations:** Upon termination, Business Associate shall:
   - Return or destroy all PHI received from Covered Entity;
   - Maintain no copies of PHI;
   - Certify to Covered Entity that all PHI has been returned or destroyed.

## ARTICLE 5 - MISCELLANEOUS

5.1 **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of [State], without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

5.2 **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

5.3 **Amendments:** This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

5.4 **Survival:** The obligations of Business Associate under Article 4.4 shall survive the termination of this Agreement.
